# Conversion Funnels, Pricing Psychology & Community Distribution Reference

## 1. Dual-Funnel Sales Channel Architecture

When marketing an adblocker, traffic should be routed into one of two high-converting funnels depending on user temperature and channel:

### Funnel A: Product-Led Extension/App Install $\rightarrow$ Welcome Tab Upsell (Best for Organic, SEO, Reddit)
1. **Landing Page / Store Listing**: 1-click free install (zero signup friction; achieves 25%–45% visitor-to-install conversion).
2. **Instant Activation (`onInstalled` Welcome Tab)**:
   - Step 1: Show live confirmation (*"Protection Active: 34,000+ filter rules loaded in 12ms"*).
   - Step 2: Run an automatic 3-second browser privacy check showing what *basic* mode blocks vs. what **Pro Mode** unlocks (e.g., YouTube/Twitch Turbo Shield, System-Wide DNS, Malware Guard, Multi-Device Sync).
   - Step 3: Present a **48-Hour New User Offer** (£19.99/year or £39.99 Lifetime).
3. **In-Product Milestone Triggers**:
   - **Day 3 / 500 Ads Blocked**: Toast notification inside extension popup showing time/data saved + 1-click trial of Pro features.
   - **Day 7**: End-of-trial summary email/notification showing exact trackers blocked during the week.

### Funnel B: Direct-to-Checkout Interactive Diagnostic Quiz (Best for Paid Cold Traffic on Native/Social)
1. **Landing Page**: Interactive 20-second *"Browser Speed & Tracker Exposure Scan"*.
2. **Diagnostic Result**: Displays user's OS, browser, detected ad-script vulnerability score, and estimated bandwidth wasted per month.
3. **Direct Offer**: Risk-free 30-day money-back guarantee on the Annual Pro Plan (£19.99 first year) or 7-day trial with instant download.

## 2. Pricing Tier Optimization for Rapid Cashflow Reinvestment

To turn a **£10/day (£300/month)** budget into a self-funding growth engine, you must optimize for **Day-1 Cash Collected (Upfront LTV)** rather than slow monthly drip:

| Tier | Recommended Price (GBP) | Target Buyer Persona | Role in Reinvestment Engine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free Core** | £0 | Viral & ASO Engine | Drives Chrome Web Store rank, reviews, and word-of-mouth referrals. |
| **Pro Monthly** | £2.99 / mo | Skeptical Testers | High-price anchor that makes the Annual plan look like a no-brainer (44% savings). |
| **Pro Annual (Hero)** | **£19.99 / yr** (£1.66/mo) | Mainstream Users | **Primary cashflow driver.** Collects £19.99 on Day 1 (~£18.80 net after Stripe fees). |
| **Pro Family / Lifetime** | **£44.99 one-time** (up to 5 devices) | Privacy Enthusiasts / Redditors | **High-AOV booster.** Privacy users hate recurring subscriptions; 20%–30% of buyers will choose Lifetime, boosting blended AOV to ~£26+. |

## 3. Affiliate & Micro-Creator Commission Structure

At low daily ad budgets, revenue-share partnerships require **£0 upfront spend**:
- Offer **40%–50% recurring/upfront commission** via LemonSqueezy, Paddle, or Rewardful to privacy bloggers, Linux/macOS/Windows optimization YouTubers, and browser comparison sites.
- Provide affiliates with a custom co-branded landing page (`yourdomain.com/partnerName`) that automatically applies a 20% reader discount.
