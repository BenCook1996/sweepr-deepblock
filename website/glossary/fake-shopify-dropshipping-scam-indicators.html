Fake Online Shop & Dropshipping Scam Indicators

Technical Definition

Fake online shop scams are fraudulent e-commerce storefronts—often promoted via social media ads claiming a "Heritage Boutique Closing Down Sale — 80% Off"—that ship counterfeit goods or steal payment details.

How SWEEPR DeepBlock Mitigates It

SWEEPR DeepBlock evaluates storefront DOM heuristics in real time: fabricated countdown timers,cloned review stars without verified merchant IDs, Newly Registered Domain (NRD) patterns, and missing company registration disclosures.
