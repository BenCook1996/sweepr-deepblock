Header Bidding & OpenRTB Latency

Technical Definition

Header bidding (typically powered by Prebid.js and OpenRTB endpoints) is a programmatic auction where a webpage simultaneously queries 15 to 40 Supply-Side Platforms (PubMatic, Rubicon, Criteo, AppNexus, Index Exchange) before rendering page content.

How SWEEPR DeepBlock Mitigates It

Each auction round saturates browser main-thread CPU and network sockets. Blocking OpenRTB auction endpoints at the MV3 DNR layer eliminates 3 to 5 seconds of page-load delay on news and recipe sites.
