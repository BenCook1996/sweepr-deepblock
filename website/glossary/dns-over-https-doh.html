DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH)

Technical Definition

DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH, RFC 8484) is a security protocol that encrypts Domain Name System (DNS) resolution queries inside standard HTTPS TLS traffic on port 443 rather than sending plaintext UDP packets on port 53.

How SWEEPR DeepBlock Mitigates It

Without DoH, your ISP or public Wi-Fi operator can log every hostname your browser resolves even when visiting HTTPS websites. Pairing encrypted DoH resolvers with SWEEPR DeepBlock prevents ISP domain logging.
