Self-Serve Troubleshooting & Diagnostics Matrix

Resolve common activation, device-seat, filter-rule, and RAM hibernation questions in under two minutes without waiting for a ticket reply.

1. Activation & Zero-Copy Sync

Activating Your SWEEPR-DB Key

After checkout, your SWEEPR-DB-... license key auto-syncs into the extension from the confirmation screen or the Customer Portal (via a 6-digit email OTP). If your browser is offline, the extension maintains a 7-day offline grace window and refreshes status every 12 hours.

2. Device Seat Management

Managing Your 5-Device Quota

Every Pro subscription covers up to 5 simultaneous browsers or devices across Chrome, Edge, Firefox (Desktop & Android), Apple Safari (macOS & iOS), Brave, and Opera. If you replace a laptop or reinstall an OS and see a seat-limit warning, log into the Customer Portal or submit an Activation Code / Key ticket above to clear an old browser slot.

3. Site Layout & Anti-Adblock

Fixing False Positives or Broken Elements

If a site blocks access or hides a legitimate widget, press Alt+Shift+S (Mac: Ctrl+Shift+S) to pause protection on that host, or use the 1-click Element Zapper to remove a stubborn overlay per-host. Run our Free Adblock Test Lab to verify your browser's filter rules are active.

4. Video & Cookie Pop-Up Rules

YouTube, Twitch & Stubborn CMP Banners

SWEEPR Deepblock v1.4.0 ships 36,019 static C++ DNR rules plus 100 over-the-air dynamic rules (/api/filters/delta) and handles 150+ CMPs automatically without scroll lock. If a new regional banner or pre-roll script slips through, send us the URL under Technical Support so our engineers can push an OTA rule.

5. Memory Optimization

Total Tab & RAM Tamer Exclusions

Total Tab & RAM Tamer uses Chrome's native chrome.tabs.discard API to hibernate background tabs after 10 minutes of inactivity, freeing unused tab process memory while automatically protecting active tabs, pinned tabs, audio-playing tabs, and tabs with unsaved forms. Press Alt+Shift+Z anytime to snooze idle tabs on demand.

6. Keyboard & URL Stripper

Shortcuts & Tracking Parameter Cleanup

Use Alt+Shift+D (Mac: Ctrl+Shift+D) to open the dashboard anytime. Our built-in queryTransform rule strips 229 tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid, ttclid) automatically in C++ before pages load.

Support & Account FAQ

Do I need an account to install and use the extension?

No. Installing SWEEPR Deepblock from Chrome Web Store, Edge Add-ons, Mozilla Add-ons, Opera Add-ons, or Apple Safari requires zero account, email, or payment card—every fresh install automatically starts a no-card 7-day full Pro trial (full ad/tracker blocking, YouTube Shield, 160-CMP cookie auto-rejection + scroll unlock, OTA Delta updates, Total Tab & RAM Tamer, and 5-device sync). After day 7, Pro modules gate until you activate a Pro license via Customer Portal (email + 6-digit OTP).

Why does SWEEPR Deepblock request <all_urls> host access?

Browsers require <all_urls> solely so local MV3 rules and content scripts can block ads/trackers, auto-reject GDPR cookie banners, run the Element Zapper, and strip URL tracking parameters locally on any site you visit. Zero browsing history is ever logged or uploaded.

Where do I find my SWEEPR-DB license key if I lost the email?

Go to the Customer Portal, enter your checkout email address, and verify with the 6-digit code sent to your inbox. Your active SWEEPR-DB-... key and billing status display immediately.

How can I test if my adblocker is actually blocking trackers?

Run our Live Adblock Test Lab, estimate tab memory with the Browser RAM Calculator, and review v1.4.0 Benchmarks.

How do I refresh filter rules or uninstall safely?

To pause on one site without uninstalling, press Alt+Shift+S (Mac: Ctrl+Shift+S). To update or remove the extension cleanly, open chrome://extensions (or about:addons in Firefox).

Can I use one license across Chrome on Windows and Firefox on Android?

Yes. Your single Pro license covers up to 5 browsers across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS (Safari Web Extension), and Firefox for Android. Install via our Download Hub and enter the same key on each browser.
