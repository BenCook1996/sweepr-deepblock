Frequently Asked Questions

What tracking parameters does SWEEPR DeepBlock strip automatically?

SWEEPR DeepBlock strips utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, fbclid, gclid, msclkid, dclid, mc_eid, igshid, twclid, and _hsenc in sub-millisecond time.

How does Encrypted DNS (DoH) protect against CNAME tracker cloaking?

First-party CNAME cloaking disguises third-party analytics trackers under a website’s own subdomain. SWEEPR DeepBlock combines network declarativeNetRequest filtering with encrypted DNS rules to block disguised tracker endpoints.
