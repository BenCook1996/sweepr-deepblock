Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: November 2026 • SWEEPR Global Limited

🔒

100% Local On-Device Blocking Guarantee

SWEEPR Deepblock blocks ads, closes cookie pop-ups, strips URL trackers, and inspects pages locally inside your browser. While we collect minimal account, billing, and website analytics data on sweeprdeepblock.com to operate our service (disclosed in full below), our browser extension never uploads, reads, stores, or sells your browsing history, search queries, form inputs, passwords, or personal files.

1. User Data We Collect (And What We Never Collect)

Yes, we collect limited user data on our website and licensing servers. We practice strict data minimization and only collect the user data required to deliver your software license, authenticate your account, process payments, provide customer support, and measure aggregate website performance:

  • Account, Licensing & Authentication Data: When you purchase a Pro subscription, activate a trial, or sign in to the Customer Portal (/login), we collect your email address, subscription plan tier, one-time passcode (OTP) login verification records, and a one-way cryptographic hardware/device identifier hash (used solely to enforce your 5-device seat limit).
  • Billing, Currency & Payment Data: When you check out, we collect your billing country, currency preference, and transaction status. All credit/debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, and PayPal payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, Inc. or PayPal under PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption—SWEEPR never sees or stores your raw card number or CVV.
  • Website Analytics, Cookies & Attribution Data: When you visit sweeprdeepblock.com, we collect aggregate usage telemetry (pages visited, referral URLs, browser/OS type, approximate country derived from IP address, and ad-campaign conversion events) using first-party session storage and third-party analytics/attribution cookies disclosed in Section 4.
  • Customer Support & Diagnostic Inquiries: When you contact us via our Support Center or email, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message so we can resolve your inquiry.
  • What Your Browser Extension Never Collects or Sends Us (No Browsing History Logged — Local-First Engine): The SWEEPR Deepblock browser extension never transmits the URLs you visit, domain names, webpage content, search engine queries, cookies, passwords, or private browsing data to our servers.
1A. Anonymous Extension Operational Telemetry (6 Non-Identifying Lifecycle Events)

For 100% verifiable transparency, our extension operates on a No Browsing History Logged — Local-First Engine architecture and sends only 6 anonymous, non-identifying operational lifecycle events to /api/telemetry to verify extension health and permission onboarding:

  • extension_installed — Sent once on initial install (includes extension version and browser family only).
  • protection_active — Sent once when declarativeNetRequest rulesets and host permissions are confirmed active.
  • first_value_seen — Sent once when the extension blocks its first ad/cookie banner or completes a diagnostic check.
  • welcome_tab_choice — Sent when you select an onboarding mode on the local welcome screen.
  • milestone_reached — Sent when your local aggregate block counter crosses a milestone threshold (e.g., 100, 1,000, or 10,000 items blocked — contains no site or domain names).
  • heartbeat — A weekly anonymous health ping confirming extension version and active ruleset status.

None of these 6 events ever include a visited URL, hostname, IP log, search query, or personal identifier.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the user data we collect exclusively to:

  • Deliver your 6-digit activation code, extension license key, and cryptographically signed Ed25519 license token.
  • Send transactional billing receipts, trial/renewal notices, OTP login codes, and customer support replies.
  • Verify active subscription status and manage your up to 5 licensed devices or browsers.
  • Display accurate regional pricing (GBP, USD, EUR) and measure aggregate website traffic and marketing campaign conversions on sweeprdeepblock.com.

3. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Automated Heuristics

Yes, we use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine-assisted heuristic systems across our threat-intelligence pipeline, in-browser protection modules, and customer operations:

  • On-Device AI Slop & Scam Store Detection: SWEEPR Deepblock uses AI-trained heuristic classifiers and pattern-scoring rules that execute 100% locally inside your browser to detect and filter low-quality AI-generated content farms ("AI Slop") on search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo) and to flag fraudulent e-commerce scam stores and manipulated product reviews before you shop.
  • Automated AI Ad-Filter Synthesis Pipeline: Our backend engineering pipeline uses automated headless browsers and AI-assisted DOM analysis across 1,000+ public websites daily to identify new ad-injection scripts, cookie consent walls, and tracking parameters. Those findings are compiled into static declarativeNetRequest filter rules delivered to your extension.
  • AI-Assisted Customer Support & Diagnostics: We may use AI-assisted tools internally to help triage support inquiries, summarize technical bug reports, and improve documentation accuracy.
  • Strict AI Privacy Guarantee (Zero AI Training on User Data): We never transmit your browsing history, webpage DOM contents, search queries, or personal data to external AI providers, and we never use customer personal data to train or fine-tune external AI or large language models (LLMs).

4. Third-Party Data Collectors, Analytics & Named Sub-Processors

Yes, we use third-party data collectors and infrastructure sub-processors on our website (sweeprdeepblock.com) and billing backend. The SWEEPR Deepblock browser extension itself contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or trackers. On our website and licensing backend, we share or process limited data only with the following named third parties:

  • Google LLC (Google Analytics 4 — Measurement ID G-01MNBQSL9K): Used on sweeprdeepblock.com to collect aggregate website traffic, page views, session duration, and checkout conversion telemetry. You can opt out at any time using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking analytics scripts in your browser.
  • Stripe, Inc. & PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A.: Third-party PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processors that collect and process your payment card details, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, PayPal account details, billing address, and fraud-prevention signals during checkout.
  • Advertising & Conversion Attribution Providers (Marketing Website Only): To measure the performance of our own paid advertising campaigns on our public website and checkout pages, we may load conversion attribution pixels from Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Bing Ads — bat.bing.com), Reddit, Inc. (redditstatic.com / alb.reddit.com), Meta Platforms, Inc. (connect.facebook.net), and OpenAI, L.L.C. (ads.openai.com). These scripts operate strictly on sweeprdeepblock.com and never run inside the browser extension.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party marketers.

5. Browser Extension Permissions & Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Every permission requested in our browser extension manifest (manifest.json) is strictly scoped to a single user-facing privacy, adblocking, or performance feature:

  • declarativeNetRequest & declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: Used to block ad networks, third-party trackers, crypto-mining scripts, and URL tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid) at the browser network layer without reading page content.
  • storage: Stores your site allowlist, blocked-tracker counters, and license key locally on your device.
  • tabs & system.memory: Used by the Total Tab & RAM Tamer module to display your live browser memory gauge and automatically hibernate idle background tabs while skipping active audio, video, or unsaved forms.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): Required so local content scripts can automatically click "Reject All" on cookie pop-ups, hide AI spam search results, and warn you about fake scam online shops.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure: The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome Extension APIs adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never transfer, sell, or use extension data for personalized advertising or creditworthiness lending.

6. Data Retention & Security

All communication between the SWEEPR Deepblock license verifier and our licensing API is encrypted via TLS 1.3, and license tokens are cryptographically signed using Ed25519 keys. Billing records are retained only as long as your subscription is active or as required by UK/EU tax and accounting regulations.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR, UK DPA 2018 & CCPA

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the account and billing records associated with your email address.
  • Correction & Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account data and de-registration of your device hashes.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics & No Sale of Personal Information: Opt out of website analytics cookies and confirm that SWEEPR Global Limited never sells, rents, or trades personal information to third parties or data brokers.

To exercise any of your privacy rights, please submit a request via our Support Center or email support@sweeprdeepblock.com.
