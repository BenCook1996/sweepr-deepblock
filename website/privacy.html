Privacy Policy & Browser Extension Data Disclosure

SWEEPR Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SWEEPR Deepblock browser extension and website (https://sweeprdeepblock.com). We built SWEEPR Deepblock on a strict local-first privacy architecture: we never log, collect, transmit, or sell your browsing history, search queries, or web page contents.

1. Single Purpose & Local Browser Processing

All ad blocking, tracker filtering, automatic GDPR/CCPA cookie consent rejection, search result spam filtering, and fake online shop heuristics execute 100% locally inside your browser using Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest rules and local DOM scripts. No URLs you visit are ever sent to our servers.

2. Browser Extension Permissions Justification

  • declarativeNetRequest & declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: Used exclusively to block third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, cryptominers, and URL tracking parameters locally inside the browser.
  • storage & alarms: Used to store your local feature toggles, paused-site whitelist, local block counters, and periodic subscription status checks in chrome.storage.local.
  • tabs & system.memory: Used strictly by the optional Browser Speed & Tab Hibernation feature (chrome.tabs.discard) to snooze inactive background tabs and save local crash-recovery tab snapshots.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): Required so the extension can block ads, auto-reject cookie banners, and display fake-shop safety warnings on the websites you visit.

3. Subscription Licensing & Payment Data

When you purchase or activate a paid SWEEPR Deepblock Pro subscription:

  • Payments: Processed directly by Stripe using PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption. SWEEPR Technologies never sees or stores your full credit/debit card number.
  • License Verification: When you enter a License Key or 6-digit Activation Code in the extension, the extension sends only your license key and an anonymous random installation identifier (ext_db_...) to https://sweeprdeepblock.com/api/license-verify to confirm that your subscription is active.

4. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Compliance

The use of information received from Google APIs and browser extension APIs adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer user data to third parties for advertising, creditworthiness, or data brokering.

5. Contact & Data Deletion Requests

For any privacy inquiries, subscription cancellation, or account deletion requests, contact us at support@sweeprdeepblock.com or manage your subscription anytime in the Customer Portal.
