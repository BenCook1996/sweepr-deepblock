What Runs Locally in v1.4.0

SWEEPR Deepblock compiles filtering straight into your browser's native C++ declarativeNetRequest pipeline so ad auctions, cryptominers, and tracking beacons are dropped before a network socket ever opens.

  • 36,019 Bundled Static DNR Rules (5 Rulesets): 20,000 EasyList ad & tracker rules (+ 50 anti-breakage allow rules) + 12,000 EasyPrivacy + 4,000 Regional DE/FR/ES + 18 cryptominer rules + 1 queryTransform rule stripping 229 tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, msclkid, ttclid).
  • 100 OTA Dynamic Rules + 100 Cosmetic Selectors: Fast over-the-air delta updates (OTA delta ruleset synced via /api/filters/delta) plus 391 ABP cosmetic and video-player rules without waiting for store review cycles.
  • Automated Cookie Auto-Reject & Scroll Unlock: Deep Shadow-DOM traversal (queryAllDeep) clicks "Reject All" across 160 CMP frameworks (OneTrust, Cookiebot, Usercentrics, Didomi, Quantcast) and restores overflow: auto.
  • Total Tab & RAM Tamer: Automatically hibernates idle background tabs after 10 minutes via native chrome.tabs.discard (freeing inactive tab process memory while protecting active, pinned, audio, and form tabs).

Download & Installation FAQ

Is the extension download free, and do I need an account?

Installing SWEEPR Deepblock from Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons, Mozilla Add-ons, Opera Add-ons, or Safari is free with no account, email, or payment card required. Every fresh install begins a 7-day full Pro trial with active ad blocking, YouTube Shield, 160-CMP auto-rejection, 229 tracking parameters stripped, OTA Delta updates, and Total Tab & RAM Tamer; after 7 days, Pro modules gate until you activate a Pro key (from £2.50/mo eq.).

Why does the extension ask for <all_urls> permission?

Browsers require <all_urls> host access so local declarativeNetRequest rules, cookie banner auto-rejectors, and the Element Zapper can run on whichever websites you visit. Everything executes 100% locally in-browser—zero visited URLs, search queries, or browsing history are ever logged or uploaded.

Should I install Firefox for Android or the Android .apk?

For full mobile ad blocking, YouTube shielding, and GDPR cookie auto-rejection on Android, install our signed add-on inside Firefox for Android via Mozilla Add-ons. The Android Companion (.apk) is an optional status helper for managing your license on mobile.

Does SWEEPR Deepblock work on Brave, Arc, Vivaldi, Thorium, or Floorp?

Yes. Chromium-based browsers (Brave, Arc, Vivaldi, Thorium, Ungoogled Chromium) install from the Chrome Web Store listing, while Gecko-based forks (Floorp, Zen, LibreWolf, Waterfox) install via Mozilla Add-ons. Note that Kiwi Browser on Android was archived in Jan 2025—see our Kiwi to Firefox Android migration guide and all Browser Setup Guides.

What is the status of Apple Safari on macOS and iPhone/iPad?

SWEEPR Deepblock v1.4.0 ships a native Safari Web Extension for macOS and iOS (App Store submission in progress alongside Chrome, Edge, Firefox 128.0+, Brave, and Opera), covered under your 5-device license and 7-day no-card Pro trial.

Always install from the official marketplace links on this page or our signed /download/* installers. One Pro license supports up to 5 active browsers/devices simultaneously; if you replace a computer, sign in at the Customer Portal or contact Support to view your key and manage seats.
