Enable in Safari on iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the iOS Settings app.
  2. Scroll to Apps → Safari → Extensions (or Safari → Extensions).
  3. Tap SWEEPR DeepBlock and toggle Allow Extension on.
  4. Set website permissions to Allow for All Websites to enable ad, tracker, and cookie auto-reject protection.
